Fanos is a reader for web novels.

<b>Your library</b>
• Novels from the sites you read, checked for new chapters in the background, with a notification when some arrive
• Sections, filters and search, and a calendar of when each novel usually updates
• Chapters downloaded for reading offline

<b>The reader</b>
• Turn pages or scroll; themes, fonts (or your own) and text size
• A built-in dictionary, highlights, notes, bookmarks and quote cards to share
• Footnotes as pop-ups, pictures full screen, a clock and battery at the foot of the page

<b>Listening</b>
• Chapters read aloud with your phone's voices, or with natural voices that work offline

<b>Sites come as extensions</b>
Fanos comes with no sources. Browse › Extensions lists the published ones (Royal Road, Scribble Hub, Novel Updates and Patreon): install the ones you read.

<b>Private by design</b>
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your library stays on your phone and in your own backups.
